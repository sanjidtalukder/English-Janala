Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I memorize English vocabulary effectively?

The best way is through Active Recall & Spaced Repetition rather than rote memorization:

  • Learn 5-10 words daily and use them in full sentences.
  • Always listen to the audio pronunciation and speak the word out loud.
  • Review words through our আজকের Revision feature at 1, 3, and 7-day intervals.
2. How does the Spaced Repetition (Revision) system work?

When you mark a word as Complete Learning, our intelligent schedule sets a review date. When a word becomes due, it appears in your Revision queue. Getting quiz answers correct increases the review interval (1 day → 3 days → 7 days → 14 days), locking the word into your long-term memory permanently.

3. What is the difference between Meaning and Pronunciation?

Meaning: The concept or definition of the word in your native language (Bengali) so you understand what it signifies.

Pronunciation: The exact phonetics and sound of how native English speakers say the word. Listening to the audio helps you speak with natural cadence and accent.

4. Why should I practice with sentences and dialogues?

Learning isolated words often leads to forgetting them quickly in real life. When you click the info (ℹ️) icon, our practice section shows 3 contextual sentences and a short dialogue turn. Seeing how a word interacts with prepositions and verbs prepares you for real-world English conversations.

5. How does the Daily Goal and Streak tracker help me?

Consistency is key to language learning. Studying just 15 minutes every day is far more effective than 3 hours once a week. Your daily streak rewards your consistency and keeps you motivated to achieve fluency.
